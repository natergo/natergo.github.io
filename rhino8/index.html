How to Obtain Rhino 8 Commercial License

  1. Press ⊞ Win, search for Rhino 8, right-click → Uninstall.
  2. Open Settings → Apps → Installed Apps, search Rhino 8, click the 3 dots on the right, click uninstall, restart if prompted.
  3. Install the latest Rhino installer from: https://www.rhino3d.com/download/rhino/latest, open the Installer and install
  4. After installation, open "Windows Defender Firewall with Advanced Security"
  5. Click "Inbound Rules" on the Top Left
  6. Click "New Rules" under the Actions section on the Top Right
  7. Follow the following:
      RULE TYPE: Choose "Program" option
      PROGRAM: Copy paste "C:\Program Files\Rhino 8\System\Rhino.exe"
      ACTION: Choose "Block the connection"
      PROFILE: Leave all checkboxes TICKED
      NAME: Name it "Rhino 8"
  8. THIS IS IMPORTANT! Click Outbound Rules, repeat Steps 5-6
  9. Open Rhino 8 (you will see a prompt to enter your email)
  10. Enter your email & tick the "I have read & agree to bla bla bla" checkbox, click "Continue"
  11. You wil see a "Rhino cannot connect" page. This is fine. Click proceed with an offline validation
  12. Enter the following license key: RH80-Q9R0-4A8G-8RAJ-0H9H-88BG
  13. You will see "Get Validation Code Manually". Do NOT follow their steps or instructions. Click continue
  14. Enter your Validation Code: A9F1-31EE-60C6-0001-1213, click Continue
    15. Congrats, enjoy your lifetime Rhino 8.
    As long as you do not unblock Rhino from the Windows Defender Firewall, Rhino will never be able to revalidate your license key with the License Manager & Servers.
